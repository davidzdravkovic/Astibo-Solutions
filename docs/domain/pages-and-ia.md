# Pages and information architecture

Keep the first version focused. Every page should help a suitable client understand the offer, trust the person doing the work, or take the next step.

## Proposed sitemap
- **Home:** audience/problem, core offer, service overview, working approach, real proof if available, primary CTA.
- **Services:** websites/web apps, MVP/product development, mobile, UX/UI, consulting; explain fit, outputs, and boundaries.
- **Work / Projects:** verified projects only. Clearly distinguish client work, personal projects, demos, and experiments.
- **About:** who will do the work, relevant experience and approach—only verified facts.
- **Contact / Book a call:** short inquiry form and/or discovery-call booking.

## Navigation (CONFIRMED for v1)
Home · Services · Work · About · Contact

Primary CTA: **Discuss a project** or **Book a discovery call** (choose one main CTA after booking is decided).

## Page rules
- Do not create separate pages for every technology or industry without a clear buyer need.
- Do not publish empty case-study pages or fabricated client logos.
- If there is not enough verified work for a portfolio page, use a smaller “Selected projects” section with accurate labels or omit it.
- Keep contact and booking accessible from key pages.
