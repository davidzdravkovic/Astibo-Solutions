# Domain documentation

These files define Astibosoft's business context and the required behavior of its marketing site. Read only the files relevant to the task; do not automatically load every file for unrelated work.

## How to use them
- `company.md`, `audience.md`, `services.md`: who the business is for and what it offers.
- `engagement-model.md`: how a potential client becomes a project and how work continues.
- `pages-and-ia.md`: pages, navigation, and each page's purpose.
- `contact-form.md`, `discovery-booking.md`: lead capture and scheduling behavior.
- `content-truth.md`, `constraints.md`: evidence, boundaries, and claims that must not be made.
- `tech-preferences.md`: implementation preferences, not confirmed requirements unless marked as decided.

Related: `docs/research/` holds evidence-backed decisions (skill: `website-evidence-research`). Domain files are the business source of truth; research files record *why* a website choice was made.

## Status labels
- **CONFIRMED** — supplied or verified fact.
- **PROPOSED** — a sensible starting recommendation; owner approval may be needed.
- **OPEN** — owner must decide.
- **NONE** — no verified information is available; do not invent it.

## Research basis
Service categories are informed by [Afillix](https://www.afillix.com/): UX/UI design, web development, mobile development, and MVP development. Use this only as a category reference; do not copy its wording, claims, testimonials, or positioning. The recommended structure emphasizes a clear offer, a credible working approach, verified proof, and an easy next step—without presenting a solo provider as a large agency.

References:
- Afillix, service categories: https://www.afillix.com/
- Afillix's public service structure supports the categories above; its team-size, client, and result claims are not transferable to Astibosoft.

This is a starting specification, not legal advice or proof that any proposed service or tool has been selected.
