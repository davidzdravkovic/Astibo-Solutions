# Constraints and non-negotiable rules

- **Truth:** never invent business facts, client results, reviews, team size, credentials, partnerships, metrics, or guarantees.
- **Operating model (internal):** the business is one person. Use that as a constraint and reality for capacity, claims, and research—not as a public marketing theme. Do not invent a team, offices, or agency capacity. Do not lead the site with “one engineer / one person” messaging unless the owner asks for that.
- **Scope:** market only services the owner confirms they can deliver and has capacity to accept.
- **No copying:** Afillix is a reference for service categories only. Do not copy its text, visual identity, testimonials, claims, or case studies.
- **Tone:** clear, specific, calm, professional, and human. Avoid inflated agency language, empty buzzwords, and generic AI-style claims.
- **No competitor attacks:** do not disparage competitors or make unsupported comparisons.
- **Privacy:** request only necessary lead information; protect submissions and secrets; do not publish a privacy/legal claim without review.
- **Legal compliance:** identify applicable business, privacy, cookie, and consumer requirements before launch. This document is not legal advice.
- **Language:** the site is English only for version 1 (**CONFIRMED** by owner); do not add translations without approval.
- **Commercial promises:** no fixed prices, delivery guarantees, availability promises, or support commitments until confirmed.
- **Missing information:** if a fact is unknown, mark it **OPEN** or omit it; do not fill gaps by guessing.
