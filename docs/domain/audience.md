# Audience

## Primary audience (PROPOSED)
Small and medium-sized businesses that are growing, improving an existing operation, or launching a digital product and need direct access to a capable software engineer.

## Likely buyer situations
- A business needs a professional website or customer-facing web experience.
- Manual work or disconnected tools are creating friction.
- A founder or business lead wants to test a product idea with an MVP before investing more.
- An existing website or application needs UX, code-quality, architecture, performance, or maintainability improvements.
- A company needs short-term specialist help without hiring a full-time engineer.

## Buying triggers
A new service or product launch, outdated digital experience, repeated operational pain, a stalled build, or uncertainty about technical direction.

## Not a fit (PROPOSED)
- Buyers seeking the lowest price regardless of quality.
- Projects with unclear ownership, impossible deadlines, or no access to decision-makers.
- Requests for guaranteed business outcomes or unlimited work for a fixed fee.
- Work outside the owner's actual skills or available capacity.

Do not claim a specific industry niche or proven industry expertise until the owner confirms it.
