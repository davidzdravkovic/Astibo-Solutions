# Engagement model

## Proposed flow
1. **Initial conversation:** understand the business problem, users, constraints, budget range, and desired outcome.
2. **Discovery / scoping:** agree on requirements, priorities, risks, and what is deliberately out of scope.
3. **Written proposal:** define deliverables, milestones, responsibilities, assumptions, price, and payment terms.
4. **Build in increments:** share progress regularly, demonstrate working results, and request feedback at agreed checkpoints.
5. **Handover:** provide agreed code, deployment details, documentation, and knowledge transfer.
6. **Optional follow-up:** maintenance, improvements, or ongoing engineering support under a separate agreement.

## Working principles (PROPOSED)
- Direct communication with the person doing the work.
- Small, reviewable milestones and explicit scope.
- Raise trade-offs and risks early; avoid promising what cannot be verified.
- Start with the smallest useful solution rather than unnecessary complexity.

## OPEN decisions
Paid or free discovery; proposal turnaround; pricing model; minimum project size; payment schedule; support period; response hours; contract and IP terms.

Do not promise a delivery date or availability before checking scope and capacity.
