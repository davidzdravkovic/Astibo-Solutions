How Astibo Solutions works.

An independent software practice for growing businesses: clear problems, written scope, incremental delivery and a handover that leaves you able to move on.

Working approach

  • Direct communication

    Questions, decisions and difficult news stay close to the work. No sales-to-delivery hand-off.

  • Small, reviewable steps

    Work is split into milestones you can look at and react to, with scope written down.

  • Trade-offs and risks early

    If something will cost more, take longer or not work the way you expect, that is raised as soon as it is known.

  • The smallest useful solution first

    Complexity is added when it earns its place, not because it is available.

Who this suits

A good fit

  • You have a real problem or a clearly defined idea, even if the solution is not clear yet.
  • Someone on your side can make decisions and give timely feedback.
  • You want to understand what is being built and why.
  • You value quality and clear scope over the lowest price.

Probably not a fit

  • Projects where nobody owns decisions on the client side.
  • Work that needs a guaranteed business outcome, or unlimited changes for a fixed fee.
  • Deadlines that cannot be met without cutting corners.
  • Work outside the skills or capacity available for the engagement.
